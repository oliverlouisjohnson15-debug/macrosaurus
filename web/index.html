Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?+
Yes, and not the kind of free that stops working in a fortnight. Core tracking, the adaptive engine, weekly check-ins, gym training and the entire game are free forever, with no card to start. Premium adds food quality scoring, unlimited AI logging, the full recipe library and body-fat scans.
Is it actually science-based?+
Yes. Your targets retune each week from your real trend weight using research-backed methods, and every change comes with the reason it happened. No black box, no mystery numbers, no being told to eat 1,200 calories.
How is it different from other trackers?+
You still get the adaptive science and quick AI logging you'd expect, plus three things most trackers skip: it reads a restaurant menu and ranks it against the day you have left, it runs your gym sessions in the same app, and it has a game that genuinely makes you want to log. And it's built for the UK. Your units and your supermarket, not just the States.
Can it help me order when I'm eating out?+
Yes. Paste the restaurant's link or photograph the menu at the table. Macrosaurus reads every dish, estimates the macros, and ranks the menu three ways: what fits the rest of your day, most protein, or lightest, and switching between them is instant. It never hides a dish for being too big: it tells you what it would cost and leaves the choice to you.
Does it track training as well as food?+
Yes. Pick the gym you're actually standing in and Macrosaurus programmes around the kit that's there. Log your sets as you go, follow each lift's progression, and a training session keeps your streak alive the same as a clean day of food. Training logging is free and always will be.
Can I import recipes from TikTok, Instagram or YouTube?+
Yes. Share a cooking video straight to Macrosaurus from TikTok, Instagram or YouTube, or paste the link. We pull out the ingredients and method, work the macros out to the gram, and save it to your library sorted by what fits your day, so you never have to pause and rewind a 30-second video again.
Do I have to weigh my food?+
No. Scan a barcode, snap the label, or photograph the plate and let AI estimate it. Weighing is optional if you want extra precision.
